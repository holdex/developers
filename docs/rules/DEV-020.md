## Structure a Rule as Problem, Solution, Acceptance Criteria

### Problem

Rules written in free form are hard to scan and impossible to check
consistently. Without a fixed shape, a reader cannot tell what breaks, what to
do about it, or how to verify they complied.

### Solution

Every rule file follows the same shape. The frontmatter `title` is the
document's top-level heading, so the body title is `##`, Problem and Solution
sit at `###`, and Acceptance Criteria nests at `####`.

```md
## Title

### Problem

### Solution

#### Acceptance Criteria
```

1. **Problem** states what is broken or what someone cannot do because of it,
   framed as a job story.
1. **Solution** states the behavior to follow, plainly. Reference related rules
   by ID where one depends on or informs another.
1. **Acceptance Criteria** is a checkable list. A reviewer can run each item
   against a real subject and get a clear pass or fail.

#### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The body has Problem, Solution, and Acceptance Criteria in that order
- [ ] The Problem is a job story, not a restatement of the title
- [ ] Every acceptance item is objectively checkable against a subject
