## Problem

Anyone with admin on a company account can edit its bio on the platform, so the
accounts drift apart and nobody can tell which version is current.

## Solution

This rule is the only place the company-account copy and ownership are edited.
Merge a change here before changing a platform, then apply the reviewed text to
every affected account and record completion in the marketing Problem or PR that
scheduled the update. It lives in this public repo so every member, not only
marketing, can read it and check a live account against it.

Three rules shape the copy:

1. **The identity line.** Wherever a line stands alone (display name, tagline,
   bio), Holdex is `Design and technology studio for AI and fintech startups`.
   "Studio" answers what we are in one second.
1. **The audience.** "Startups" names the target market, while "founders" names
   the people we work with. Use "companies" only when referring to portfolio
   companies or reporting proof about them.
1. **Partner is shown, not claimed.** Describe the work: we build alongside the
   team and stay involved after launch. Do not use "partner" as a label we award
   ourselves.

Numbers follow the figure the marketing docs use. When a figure changes, update
every field that carries it in the same PR, then apply the change to the live
accounts together. The current company mark and source design files live in
[Holdex Branding](https://github.com/holdex/marketing/blob/main/docs/brand/BRANDING.md).
