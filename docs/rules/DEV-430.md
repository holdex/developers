## Deliver Bug-Free Work; Review Is a Safety Check

### Problem

Treating reviewers as the QA team pushes defects downstream, slows everyone, and
lets subjective debates block otherwise sound work.

### Solution

Own the quality of what you deliver.

1. Deliver bug-free software. Do not rely on review to catch defects you could
   have caught yourself.
1. Treat reviewers as a final safety check, not a QA team.
1. Push back on subjective feedback rather than absorbing it as a blocker.

#### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Work is delivered bug-free, not dependent on review to find defects
- [ ] Review is treated as a final safety check, not QA
- [ ] Subjective feedback is pushed back on rather than blocking the PR
