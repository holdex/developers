## Problem

A question on a PR with no reply reads as unseen, an answer that opens with a
greeting hides the decision, and replies posted one at a time never arrive as
one answer.

## Solution

[DEV-460](./DEV-460.md) has the author answer the reviewer before merge. The
questions also run the other way: on a Spec, the author asks stakeholders to
settle the business logic per [DEV-130](./DEV-130.md), and anyone can be tagged
on any PR. Whoever a thread asks or tags owes it a reply, and the author can
only apply an answer that is on the thread.

1. Reply on every thread that asks you something or tags you, even if the reply
   is only "Agreed."
1. Open with the decision: yes, no, or the option you choose. The reason
   follows. Skip the greeting and the lead-in.
1. When someone else holds the answer, tag them in that thread and say what you
   need from them. When the answer is not ready, say what it waits on.
1. When you disagree, say so in the thread and leave it open, so the
   disagreement stays visible. The author resolves the thread once its answer is
   in the PR.
1. Post every reply and new comment from one pass through "Start a review" in
   Files changed, then submit once with the outcome from
   [DEV-410](./DEV-410.md). The author gets one notification carrying every
   answer, instead of one per reply.

```md
Good: No. A rejected change goes back to the initiator, because only they can
      correct what they submitted.
Bad:  Hi @author, good question. There are a few ways to look at this, and it
      depends on who submitted the change, so...
```

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every thread that asks or tags you has your reply
- [ ] Each reply opens with the decision before the reason
- [ ] A thread whose answer sits with someone else tags that person
- [ ] A disagreement is stated in its thread, and the thread is left open
- [ ] Replies from one pass arrive as a single submitted review, not one review
      per reply
