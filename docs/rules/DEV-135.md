## Problem

When one person writes the Spec and another implements it, the understanding
built with stakeholders has to be handed over, and part of it is lost on the
way.

## Solution

The person who writes the Spec, or takes part in writing it, implements it with
their agents. The knowledge then stays with the person who built it with the
stakeholders, and the Spec and the stack descriptions per
[DEV-325](./DEV-325.md) carry it for anyone who picks the feature up later.

Agents now cover most of what used to need a separate developer: DevOps,
architecture, and writing requirements from user input. Hand implementation to
someone else only when the work needs experience or knowledge that agents do not
have, and say in the Goal what that knowledge is.

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The Spec's author, or a co-author, implements it
- [ ] A handover to another implementer names, in the Goal, the knowledge that
      makes it necessary
