## Problem

Starting while the Spec is only half-read, or silently disagreeing with its
business logic, produces the wrong Solution. Questions and objections surface
late, as rework.

## Solution

Before any work, get the Spec to clear and agreed, not just read.

1. Read and iterate over the Spec until the goal is fully clear.
1. Ask every question you have as a contributor, and resolve it on the Spec
   before work begins, not later.
1. Judge the business logic critically and agree with it. Understanding is not
   agreeing; if you disagree, challenge it now.
1. Settle every disagreement about what to build in the Spec PR, not in the code
   PRs that follow it.

The Spec is where the human hours go. With an agent doing the coding, code is
cheap. The architecture, the product idea, and the work with stakeholders and
sponsors are not. The Spec is where stakeholders are involved and where the
relationship with them is built: understanding what they need, being useful to
them, and earning their trust is the hard part, and it is human work. Give the
Spec as many hours and discussion rounds as it needs; a code PR gets minutes.

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The Spec has been read and iterated until the goal is clear
- [ ] Open questions are raised and resolved before any work begins
- [ ] You have agreed with the Spec's business logic, not only understood it
- [ ] Disagreements about what to build are settled in the Spec PR, not in code
      PRs
- [ ] Stakeholders are involved in the Spec, and the human hours on a Goal go
      there rather than into coding
