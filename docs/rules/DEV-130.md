## Frame Each Problem as a Job-Story Sub-Issue

### Problem

A vague problem statement such as "CSV export missing" names no actor and no
action. It cannot be prioritized against other barriers, and no one can verify
when it is resolved.

### Solution

Report each barrier to a Goal as its own Problem issue.

1. Title it `Problem: [statement]`, kept under 65 characters.
1. Open it as a sub-issue of the Goal, and link the Goal in the description.
1. Write the statement as a job story: what a specific user cannot do, or what
   is broken for them. Ask "what can [user] not do because of this problem?"
1. Give the body a `# Problem` section and a `# Solution` section describing the
   recommended approach or workaround before work begins.

#### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The issue is titled `Problem: ...`, under 65 characters
- [ ] It is a sub-issue of the Goal and links the Goal
- [ ] The statement is a job story, naming an actor and an inability
- [ ] The body has both a Problem and a Solution section
