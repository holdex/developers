## Problem

A member's public professional profiles should make their role at Holdex clear.
When those profiles use old positioning or promote unrelated companies, people
cannot tell what the member does now or how their work connects to Holdex.

## Solution

Immediately upon joining, update your GitHub and LinkedIn profiles to reflect
your role at Holdex. If you use X or Bluesky professionally, update those too.
Keep every professional profile current while you work at Holdex, without links
or positioning that suggest you represent another company.

Wizard opens this as a Problem automatically when a new member is onboarded, so
the check is triggered without anyone remembering to file it. Completing the
profile fields, and confirming they match, stays manual.

**GitHub**: first name only, bio states your role (for example "Full-stack
Engineer at @holdex"), company set to `@holdex`, location set to `localhost`,
current time hidden, email left empty, social links to
[x.com/HoldexIo](https://x.com/HoldexIo),
[holdex.io/works](https://holdex.io/works),
[holdex.io/jobs](https://holdex.io/jobs), and
[linkedin.com/company/holdex](https://linkedin.com/company/holdex), only
Holdex-related pinned repositories, and a public self-repo README explaining
what you do at Holdex and linking to relevant work.

**LinkedIn**: add Holdex as your current experience and use the
[canonical company description in DEV-530](./DEV-530.md#holdex-on-linkedin)
verbatim. You may follow it with one sentence about what you do at Holdex or
something you have built here, preferably with a name or a number that can be
verified. Do not add retired positioning such as DeFi, RWA, Web3, or "blockchain
solutions".

The headline states your role at Holdex in plain words, for example "Senior
Software Engineer at Holdex". Avoid retired verticals in it.

A member who posts under their own name also sets up the profile a reader opens
after a post or a comment, in this order:

1. **Photo**: in focus, your face, recent, and professional enough for the
   reader you want.
1. **Headline**: the outcome you create, one proof, then your title.
1. **Banner**, on a Managing Partner's profile: one line on what you build and
   for whom, with a proof row under it, right-aligned so the photo does not
   cover it on mobile. Use the
   [partner LinkedIn cover](https://www.figma.com/design/WpSncHyBcwV6Dlut2H6XsB/Marketing?node-id=1867-1240&t=n6VYnpuExbDCTBo4-1)
   on LinkedIn.
1. **About**: who you help, the problem, what Holdex does about it, the proof
   with names and numbers, and what to do next. The first sentence names the
   reader and their problem, because it is the only part LinkedIn shows before
   "see more".
1. **Featured**: three items, in order: one build story with numbers, your best
   on-positioning post, one external proof such as a talk or press mention.

The reference profile is the CEO's,
[/in/zolotokrylin](https://www.linkedin.com/in/zolotokrylin/), quoted in full in
the Reference below.

**X, when used professionally**: description states your role at @HoldexIo, link
to the [Holdex works page](https://holdex.io/works), and set the location to
`localhost`. Share Holdex news and updates when relevant, interact with the
community, and tag [@HoldexIo](https://x.com/HoldexIo).

**Bluesky, when used professionally**: bio states your role at Holdex and links
to the [Holdex works page](https://holdex.io/works). Follow and engage with
[Holdex's Bluesky presence](https://bsky.app/profile/holdex.io).

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] GitHub profile matches every field in the Solution
- [ ] LinkedIn lists Holdex as the current role with the mandatory description,
      verbatim
- [ ] No profile field describes Holdex with retired positioning (DeFi, RWA,
      Web3, blockchain solutions) or a positioning of its own
- [ ] A member who posts under their own name has a clear recent photo, a
      headline in the order outcome, proof, title, an About that opens on the
      reader's problem, and three Featured items
- [ ] If the member uses X professionally, the profile states the role, links to
      Holdex, and sets the location to `localhost`
- [ ] If the member uses Bluesky professionally, the profile states the role,
      links to Holdex, and follows Holdex's Bluesky presence

### Reference

The company description already explains what Holdex is. The optional personal
line should tell a visitor what this member actually does and what they can
trust them with. Name a product or repository when it helps, and include a
result only when it is verified. Do not fill the space with a tool inventory,
internal process language, or generic enthusiasm.

A useful line does not need a metric or a list of technologies. For example:

> I take early product ideas from rough sketches to working software.

If the entry already includes real work with verified numbers, keep that proof
after the mandatory description. While you represent Holdex, drop the "open to
work" badge, the "LinkedIn helped me get this job" line, and any headline text
about another company.

The full reference is Vadim's own profile. His headline:

> I build AI and fintech products with founders, from prototype to production
> | $140M+ raised by the companies we built | Managing Partner, Holdex

His banner reads "Building fintech and AI products with founding teams since
2017", with Clearpool, Truflation, Hex Trust, Canton Network, and $140M+ raised
underneath. His About:

> I build and scale AI and fintech startups through Holdex, the design and
> technology studio I founded in Hong Kong in 2017.
>
> Our portfolio includes Clearpool, Truflation, and TRUF.NETWORK. The companies
> we've helped build have raised more than
> $140M, and Clearpool has originated more than $960M in institutional loans.
>
> I work across product and technology, from the first decisions through launch
> and growth. I also share what I learn about running the studio, managing
> teams, and evolving how we work with AI. If you're building in AI or fintech,
> let's talk.

On GitHub, the bio gets 160 characters, which is enough:

> Full-stack Engineer at @holdex
>
> Design and Software Engineer at @holdex, a design and technology studio for AI
> and fintech startups.

The profile README lives in your self-named repo. Its opening still needs to
work in five seconds: lead with what you want to be known for, then point to one
or two things a visitor can inspect. Write about work you personally do rather
than borrowing client outcomes or lifecycle claims from the founder and company
profiles. The short Holdex descriptor provides context, but it is not the
subject. Do not repeat the full company About or turn the README into a résumé.
Skip the badges and stats widgets. For example:

> At [Holdex](https://holdex.io), a design and technology studio for AI and
> fintech startups, I design product interfaces and build them in code.
>
> [See what we've built](https://holdex.io/works).

X gives you the same 160 characters:

> Senior Software Engineer at @HoldexIo, a design and technology studio for AI
> and fintech startups.

Bluesky gives you 256 characters:

> Senior Software Engineer at Holdex, a design and technology studio for AI and
> fintech startups. I build backend systems for the products we ship with
> founders.
