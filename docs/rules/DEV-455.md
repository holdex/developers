## Problem

Agents take time to think. A developer who drives one agent thread at a time
spends most of the session waiting for it.

## Solution

Operate two or three agent threads in parallel, each on its own feature or Goal,
and cycle between them. The developer's job is directing the threads, not typing
the code, and directing well is the skill.

1. Start the first thread with a deep request: the Spec, the stack plan, and the
   constraints.
1. Start the second thread the same way, then the third.
1. Return to the first thread and direct it: answer its questions, correct its
   course, approve its next step.
1. Once it is directed, move to the next thread, and keep cycling.

Two or three is the working range. With more, the directing gets shallow; with
fewer, the developer sits idle while the agents think. The 3 to 15 minutes per
PR in [DEV-320](./DEV-320.md) count the developer's time on that PR, not the
time the agent spends thinking while the developer works another thread.

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The developer runs two or three agent threads at once, each on its own
      feature or Goal
- [ ] Each thread starts from a deep request and is directed in turn
- [ ] Waiting on an agent is spent directing another thread, not idle
