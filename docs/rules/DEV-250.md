## Problem

A meeting called for a question a thread could settle takes everyone's time at
once, and its decision never reaches anyone who was not in the room.

## Solution

Work async by default. A written question can be answered when each person is
free, and the answer stays where the next reader will look for it. One team sync
per week is the norm; anything beyond it needs a reason.

1. Raise the question in writing where it belongs, per [DEV-210](./DEV-210.md):
   the Spec, the Problem or the PR.
1. Call a meeting only when the thread has gone back and forth without
   converging, or the topic needs a live conversation, such as a conflict
   between people.
1. Write the outcome where the question started, so the decision is on record
   for everyone who was not there.

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A question is raised in writing before a meeting is called for it
- [ ] Meetings beyond the weekly sync follow a thread that did not converge, or
      a topic that needs a live conversation
- [ ] Each meeting's outcome is written where the question started
