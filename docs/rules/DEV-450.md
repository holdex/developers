## Problem

A contributor takes work without a paid AI agent, or treats exhausted credits as
a budget question, so mechanical work falls back to human hours.

## Solution

Hold a paid plan on a current AI coding agent before you take work. Free tiers
run out mid-task and are not a basis for planning. You bear the cost yourself;
see [Expenses](../EXPENSES.md) for why.

Proficiency comes with the plan. Two people on the same plan can exhaust it in a
day or stay inside the normal monthly range, and the difference is technique.
Running out is a signal to change how you prompt and scope, not a reason to ask
for a larger allowance or more hours.

What you then do with the agent is [DEV-440](./DEV-440.md): mechanical work goes
to the agent, your hours go to judgment and design.

Good: a contributor burns through a plan in a week, changes how they scope
prompts, and stays inside it after that. Bad: the same contributor asks for the
plan to be reimbursed and reports the lost time as work.

### Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AI tooling is never submitted as an expense or argued for as an allowance
- [ ] Exhausted credits are answered by a change of technique, not by a request
      for budget or extra hours
- [ ] No PR is delayed, and no mechanical work is done by hand, because the
      contributor has no working agent
